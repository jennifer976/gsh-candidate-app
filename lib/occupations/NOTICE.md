# Occupation catalogue attribution

Contains information from the O*NET® 31.0 Database, U.S. Department of Labor, Employment and Training Administration (USDOL/ETA), used under CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Source: https://www.onetcenter.org/database.html . USDOL/ETA has not approved, endorsed or tested these changes.

Global Sponsor Hub extracts codes, English titles and alternate job titles, groups aliases by code and removes duplicate aliases. Original files and hashes are recorded in catalogue.json. Descriptions, qualifications and related-role equivalences are not imported. This is a US occupation taxonomy, not a global qualifications or visa standard. Alternative multilingual input remains free text until reviewed; this catalogue does not claim translated coverage.
